SoundStage

Better sound for everything your iPhone plays.

How to start

1. Open SoundStage. 2. Play music in any app. 3. Swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center, tap the audio output button on the music controls, and choose SoundStage.

The app shows "Connected" and your sound is enhanced. You only do this once each time you start listening.

If SoundStage doesn't appear, or says "Unable to Connect"

  • Open SoundStage first, then look again.
  • Choose it while music or a podcast is playing, not a video.
  • Spotify: turn off Canvas in Spotify's settings, then close and reopen Spotify.
  • Close the app that's playing and open it again, or restart your iPhone.

In the app, Settings › How to Connect shows every step.

Plans

Try every preset and feature free for a week. Then choose a plan, or keep using Original, which plays your sound as it is. Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Restore a purchase in SoundStage › Settings › Plan › Restore Purchases. Got a code? Settings › Plan › Redeem Code.

Get help

The quickest way: in SoundStage, open Settings › Report a Problem, describe what happened and tap Send. It includes the details needed to fix it.

Or write to us here (messages there are public, so please leave out personal details).
